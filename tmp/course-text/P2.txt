ADVANCED PROGRAM IN INDUSTRIAL DATA ANALYTICS & AI (APIDA)
Program Overview
The Advanced Program in Industrial Data Analytics & AI (APIDA) is a specialized industry-focused program designed for graduates and working professionals who want to build expertise in Data Analytics, Business Intelligence, Machine Learning, and AI-driven Decision Making.
The program combines Data Management, Data Analysis, Data Visualization, Statistics, Machine Learning, and MLOps to help participants become job-ready professionals capable of solving real-world business problems using data.
APIDA is ideal for professionals who want to build strong analytics capabilities without going into advanced Deep Learning, Generative AI, and Agentic AI development.
Why APIDA?
Organizations today generate massive amounts of data and need professionals who can:
✅ Manage and process business data
✅ Analyze customer and operational performance
✅ Build insightful dashboards and reports
✅ Apply statistical techniques
✅ Develop Machine Learning models
✅ Forecast business outcomes
✅ Deploy predictive models into production
This program provides the perfect blend of Analytics, AI, and Business Intelligence skills required by modern organizations.
Program Objectives
Upon successful completion of APIDA, participants will be able to:
Manage structured and large-scale business data 
Perform advanced data analysis 
Build executive dashboards and reports 
Apply statistical techniques for business insights 
Develop predictive Machine Learning models 
Solve real-world business problems 
Deploy Machine Learning models using MLOps 
Prepare for Data Analytics and AI career opportunities
