DATA ANALYTICS SPECIALIST
Program Overview
The Data Analytics Specialist Program is a practical, industry-oriented training program designed to equip graduates and working professionals with the most in-demand skills required for modern Data Analytics and Business Intelligence careers.
This program focuses on building expertise in:
Data Management 
Data Analysis 
Data Visualization 
Business Intelligence 
Reporting Automation 
Data-Driven Decision Making 
Participants will learn how to collect, manage, analyze, visualize, and communicate business insights using industry-leading tools such as SQL Server, Python, Excel AI, and Power BI.
Why Data Analytics?
Every organization generates data and needs professionals who can convert data into actionable business insights.
Data Analytics helps organizations:
✅ Improve business performance
✅ Increase revenue
✅ Reduce costs
✅ Understand customer behavior
✅ Monitor KPIs
✅ Make informed decisions
Program Objectives
Upon successful completion of this program, participants will be able to:
Extract and manage business data 
Analyze large datasets efficiently 
Build automated reports and dashboards 
Create business intelligence solutions 
Generate actionable insights from data 
Present data effectively to stakeholders 
Prepare for Data Analyst and Business Analyst roles
